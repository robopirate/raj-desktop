## 3. Week 1: Foundation — Detailed Modules

### Day 1: Brand Bible + Canva Basics

#### Brand Bible (MUST MEMORIZE)
- **Mission:** Making every child future-ready through hands-on STEAM education
- **Target Audience:** Parents (30-45), School Principals, CSR Heads
- **Tone:** Passionate | Confident | Warm | Expert
- **Colors:**
  - Primary: #FF6B00 (Orange — energy, creativity)
  - Secondary: #1A1A2E (Navy — trust, authority)
  - Accent: #00C853 (Green — growth, success)
- **Fonts:**
  - Headlines: Montserrat Bold
  - Body: Open Sans Regular
- **NEVER USE:** Cheap, Basic, Just, Merely, Try, Hopefully, Simple
- **ALWAYS USE:** Future-ready, NEP 2020 aligned, Hands-on, Experiential, Innovation, Measurable impact

#### Canva Basics Checklist
- [ ] Create a Brand Kit (colors, fonts, logos)
- [ ] Understand frames vs elements
- [ ] Master alignment tools
- [ ] Use grids for consistent spacing
- [ ] Save templates in organized folders

#### Assignment Day 1
Recreate 3 existing RoboPirate Instagram posts exactly. Match colors, fonts, spacing.

### Day 3: Color Theory + Typography

#### Key Principles
- **Contrast:** Light text on dark background (or vice versa)
- **Hierarchy:** Headline > Subhead > Body > Caption
- **Consistency:** Same 2-3 fonts across all content
- **Accessibility:** Text readable on mobile (min 24px for body)

#### Assignment Day 3
Create 5 Instagram Story templates:
1. Quote template (for teacher/student quotes)
2. "Behind the Scenes" template
3. "Student Spotlight" template
4. Event announcement template
5. Poll/sticker template

---

### Day 4: Video Editing

#### School Video Rules
1. **Hook:** Show most exciting moment first (drone flying, robot moving)
2. **Length:** 30-60 seconds for Reels/Shorts
3. **Captions:** Always add — 85% watch without sound
4. **Branding:** Logo in corner, end card with website
5. **Music:** Upbeat, royalty-free from Canva
6. **CTA:** "DM us for a free demo" or "Link in bio"

#### Assignment Day 4
Edit 1 school video from raw footage. Must include all 6 elements above.

## 7. Daily/Weekly SOPs

### Daily SOP (30 minutes)
1. Check Buffer queue (5 min)
2. Respond to DMs/comments (10 min)
3. Check school video folder for new footage (5 min)
4. Edit 1 video OR create 2 static posts (30 min)
5. Log work in tracker sheet (5 min)

### Weekly SOP (2 hours, every Monday)
1. Review last week's analytics (15 min)
2. Plan next week's content (30 min)
3. Create holiday templates if needed (30 min)
4. Batch-create static posts (30 min)
5. Submit weekly report to Om (15 min)

### Monthly SOP (4 hours, first Monday)
1. Deep-dive analytics review (1 hour)
2. Content calendar for next month (1 hour)
3. Brand asset audit (30 min)
4. Competitor content analysis (30 min)
5. Suggest improvements to Om (1 hour)

### School Video SOP
```
STEP 1: Receive raw video from Baban/field team
STEP 2: Watch full video, mark best moments
STEP 3: Rough cut in Canva (trim to 30-60 sec)
STEP 4: Add text overlays (student name, grade, project name)
STEP 5: Add background music
STEP 6: Add logo watermark + end card
STEP 7: Write caption (use Grok for ideas, edit for brand voice)
STEP 8: Generate hashtags
STEP 9: Schedule in Buffer
STEP 10: Notify Om "[School Name] video scheduled for [Date]"
```
